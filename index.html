Captain's Log

How Seven Friends Became a Crew

Day 1, 2024. Rented turf, borrowed pads, a scoring app, and a group chat named "Turf Losers XI." We lost our first four matches by a combined 61 runs and decided the name had to go. If Luffy could break past his own ceiling and call it Gear Five, we could call our last-over hitting Gear Seven — a power that, strictly speaking, doesn't exist yet. Neither did our death-over economy, until it did.

Two years, one league title, and several suspiciously specific lbw appeals later, GEAR 7 plays every Tuesday and Thursday under lights at Grand Line Turf Arena. We still argue about DRS on a ground that doesn't have DRS.
